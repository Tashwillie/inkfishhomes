INKFISH HOMES
==============

A static website for Inkfish Homes Ltd. Each section is its own page so it can be linked, indexed and used with a keyboard or screen reader.

Pages
  /                        Home
  /what-we-do/             What we do
  /transitional-housing/   Transitional housing
  /our-schemes/            Our schemes
  /partners/               For commissioners and partners
  /our-story/              Our story
  /work-with-us/           Work with us
  /contact/                Contact and enquiry form
  /privacy/                Privacy notice
  /accessibility/          Accessibility statement
  /site-map/               Site map

The enquiry form opens the visitor's email app addressed to admin@inkfishhomes.com. No cookies are set.

Photographs belong in assets/images/ and are referenced as photo-04.jpg and photo-09.jpg through photo-14.jpg. Logos are the SVG files already in that folder.

To regenerate the HTML from scripts/render-pages.mjs:

  node scripts/render-pages.mjs
